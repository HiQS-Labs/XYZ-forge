Bugs and feature requests

Open an issue

A good report includes:

  • the harness and model involved (e.g. relay with Codex as builder),
  • the command line or skill invocation you ran,
  • what you expected and what happened instead,
  • relevant output — relay thread files, ESCALATION.md, or gate output — with anything sensitive removed.

Before filing, skim the FAQ — the two most common surprises (sandboxed runs failing quietly, and what depends_on really does) are covered there.
