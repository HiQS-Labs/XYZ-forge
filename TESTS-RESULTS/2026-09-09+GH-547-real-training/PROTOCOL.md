# Real-activity training and independently labeled holdout

Purpose: classify the described work intent of a HiQS-Labs GitHub issue/PR title. This is not completion-state verification, urgency ranking or next-action prediction. Six classes are defined in TAXONOMY.md, frozen before training.

Source: read-only Rebalance github_items cache. Deduplicate normalized titles; connect same-repository issue/PR records when titles explicitly reference their number (#N/GH-N). Retain one latest-created representative per connected family. Order families by representative created_at and form oldest 65% training pool, next 15% validation pool, newest 20% holdout pool. Sample 72/18/30 respectively with random.Random(5472), without consulting labels. Remove five previously tested exact titles from the holdout, leaving 25; do not replace them. All commits are excluded, avoiding direct PR/commit copies. Implicit relationships without explicit references can remain; this is a temporal/family-aware convenience split, not proof of total semantic deduplication or project-disjoint generalization.

Coordinator labeled train/validation. A separate model sub-agent read only the frozen taxonomy and held-out titles, not training/validation labels, predictions or earlier scores. The independently authored holdout has rationale and high/medium/low confidence. It is blinded model-based annotation, not human-verified ground truth. Keep its labels unchanged even when model predictions disagree. Freeze hashes before inference.

Train the same three methods: majority training class; word unigram/bigram TF-IDF plus logistic regression; frozen ModernBERT mean-pooled encoder plus logistic regression. C=1, max_iter=2000, random_state=547, no class weighting or hyperparameter search. Fit feature vocabulary and classifier on training only. Validation is diagnostic; do not tune on it in this round. No encoder fine-tuning. CPU four threads, eager attention, batch eight; assert no truncation. Two inference passes on holdout verify prediction repeatability, not independent samples.

Report validation and holdout accuracy, macro-F1 across all six fixed classes AND over classes actually present, per-class support/recall/confusion, uncertainty counts and high-confidence-only sensitivity without changing the headline result. Since no unclear holdout labels are present, unknown detection is untested. No calibrated probabilities, abstention or operational deployment claims. No A–F grades borrowed from the generative benchmark.

Run witnessed empty-split and cross-split duplicate controls and a fixed shuffled-training-label encoder-head comparator. Report shuffled result without asserting an exact chance bound. Require exact ID alignment with reviewer labels. Publish aggregate results, code, hashes and provenance only; raw titles and row-level labels/predictions stay under ~/.cache/xyz-modernbert-real-v2. Another operator needs authorized access to that snapshot to reproduce this private-data run.

Decision criterion: this is exploratory real-data feasibility, not production qualification. Report whether ModernBERT beats the unchanged TF-IDF baseline and which classes fail. A higher score on this changed taxonomy/corpus cannot be attributed solely to real-data training. Do not tune/relabel to manufacture a positive result.
