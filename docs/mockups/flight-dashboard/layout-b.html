Layout B. Sample repository activity, not live telemetry. Swipe horizontally with touch or a trackpad, or drag with a mouse. Use left and right arrow keys, Home, or End. Click a card to spotlight it and dim its surroundings. Click the spotlighted card again to zoom into its issues. Click the background to clear the spotlight. Escape clears a spotlight first; otherwise it returns to Layout A. Tab to a repository name and press Enter or Space to spotlight it, then again to open its issues. The close button returns to the saved Layout A.
