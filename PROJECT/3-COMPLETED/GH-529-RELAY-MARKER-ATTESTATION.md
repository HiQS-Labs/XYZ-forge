## Lessons Learned (For Future Agents)

- Trailing turn markers generated by harness scaffolding must be accounted for during prefix-based hash/signature attestation; treating trailing instructions as part of the immutable historical turn body causes false-positive refusal of valid subsequent turns.
- In-place turn insertions immediately preceding the trailing fence are structurally distinct from unauthorized historical modifications above the current turn boundary.
